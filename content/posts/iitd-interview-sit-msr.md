## Interview Experience

The interview was conducted on **7th December** Online.

There were **three professors** on the panel. One of them was Dr. Preeti Ranjan.

At the beginning, one of the professors asked:
*"Deepak, you have also applied for MS(R) CSE. What is your preference?"*

I clearly stated that my preference was **CSE**.

They then asked about my preferred subjects, and I answered:
**Operating Systems** and **Computer Architecture**.

---

### Technical Discussion

One of the professors started with an Operating Systems question:

> **On a uniprocessor system, if an infinite while loop is running, what will happen?**

I explained the concept in detail and was able to handle all the follow-up questions confidently.

---

### C Programming Questions

The discussion then shifted to C programming.

- I was asked about **Dynamic Memory Allocation** and **Dangling Pointers**.  
  Unfortunately, I wasn’t able to answer this properly as I had not revised these topics recently.

- Then I was asked about **malloc** and **calloc**.  
  I was able to answer partially, but I missed one follow-up question.

---

### Data Structures

Some questions were also asked on data structures:

- **Difference between Max Heap and Binary Search Tree (BST)**  
  I was able to answer this correctly.

---

### Conclusion

The interview concluded shortly after that.  
The professors said *“All the best”* and *“Good luck.”*

Overall, the interview was a mix of confidence and missed revision.  
It highlighted the importance of revising core C concepts along with OS fundamentals.

### Verdict

Not Selected - May be because I was already selected in MS(R) CSE.
